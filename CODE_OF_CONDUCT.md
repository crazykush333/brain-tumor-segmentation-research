# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our commitments

- Be respectful, constructive and inclusive toward everyone, regardless of background or identity.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Report scientific disagreements openly and with evidence. Never suppress, alter or selectively report results.
- Respect patient privacy and data-use agreements. Never attempt to re-identify individuals or share restricted data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and pressure to fabricate, cherry-pick or misreport results.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project owner (see `CITATION.cff`). Reports are handled confidentially. The owner may take any action appropriate to the situation, up to removal from the project.
