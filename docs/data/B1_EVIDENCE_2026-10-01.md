## 1. What this record is and is not

- **It is** the project owner's selection of the owner-approved alternative that the frozen B1 wording already permits. It does not change protocol v1.0: the research question, endpoints, grouping procedure, T_screen, split, model and statistics are untouched.
- **It is not** an external provider authorization (source class `EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_AUTHORIZATION`). No TCIA email, Help Desk reply or other provider statement is claimed.
- **It does not** cover private third-party re-hosting such as a private Kaggle dataset. Under protocol §5.1 that still needs TCIA confirmation, which has not been requested or received. The prepared inquiry ([TCIA_DATA_ROUTE_INQUIRY.md](TCIA_DATA_ROUTE_INQUIRY.md)) remains unsent and stays available for that purpose.
- **It does not** execute B2. Passing B1 only makes B2 eligible (AUTHORIZED). Nothing has been downloaded.

## 2. Route rules (owner decision, 2026-10-01)

1. Use the official TCIA BraTS 2021 source.
2. Access and download only through the official TCIA-provided route.
3. Do not create a public mirror.
4. Do not create a private Kaggle dataset mirror.
5. Do not redistribute the dataset.
6. Keep acquired research data private and access-restricted.
7. Use the data only for this academic research project.
8. Preserve the official TCIA dataset citation and DOI.
9. Do not place imaging data in GitHub.
10. Do not place patient-level data in GitHub.
11. Do not expose the acquired data through the project website (Vercel/Netlify).
12. Follow all applicable TCIA data-use restrictions and attribution requirements.

Hard rules: no Kaggle dataset upload (public or private), no persistent Kaggle mirror, no Hugging Face dataset upload, no Google Drive redistribution, no public S3 bucket, no website-hosted data, no Git LFS dataset upload, no model or demo endpoint exposing MRI data, and no sharing links to raw data. Acquired data stay in the authorized private computational environment.
