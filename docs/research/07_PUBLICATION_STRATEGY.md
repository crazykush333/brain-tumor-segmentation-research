# 07 — Publication Strategy (provisional)

The brief asks for venues to be chosen only after the contribution is clear. This document therefore gives **factual venue profiles** for the kinds of contribution in [03](03_PROJECT_OPTIONS.md). It will be revised after the direction is selected and again after results exist. **No acceptance is implied or predicted.**

Venue details (page limits, deadlines, workshop continuity) change yearly and must be checked on the official site at submission time. Entries marked [R] are from general knowledge.

| Venue | Scope | Relevant recent work (from our matrix) | Typical focus | Reproducibility / open-source expectations | Data requirements | Format [R] | Fit for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MICCAI (main)** | Medical image computing | [B2], [B5] | Methods; benchmarks accepted when rigorous ([B2]) | Code encouraged; reproducibility checklist | Public data favoured | ~8 pages + refs, LNCS | D2, D1 (if strong) |
| **UNSURE workshop @ MICCAI** | Uncertainty and safety in medical imaging | Topic match with [D1–D3] | Uncertainty, calibration, failure detection | Code encouraged | Any | LNCS short paper | D1, D3, D5 |
| **BrainLes workshop @ MICCAI** | Brain lesions, BraTS proceedings | [B3], [B8] | BraTS-related methods and analyses | Varies | BraTS | LNCS | D4, D1 |
| **MIDL** | Deep learning for medical imaging | — | Methods and validation | OpenReview; code encouraged | — | ~8 pages (full), short papers | D1, D3, D5 |
| **MELBA** | Open-access ML journal for biomedical imaging | [D1], [D10] | Thorough studies, benchmarks | Open-access, strong reproducibility culture | — | Journal, no strict limit | D1, D2, D3 |
| **Medical Image Analysis** | Top journal | [D2], [F1] | Mature, comprehensive studies | High | External validation valued | Journal | D1 or D2 if extensive |
| **IEEE TMI** | Top journal | [D5] | Methods with strong validation | High | — | Journal | D1 or D2 if extensive |
| **Radiology: Artificial Intelligence** | Clinical AI | [A5], [A6] | Clinically framed validation | Checklist (e.g. CLAIM) | External validation expected | Journal | D4, clinically framed D1 |
| **Scientific Reports / Brain Communications** | Broad / clinical neuro | [C8], [C6] | Clinically relevant empirical studies | Data/code availability statements | — | Journal | Clinically framed D1/D2 |

Notes:

- Before submission, read 3–5 recent papers from the chosen venue to calibrate expected depth.
- Reporting guidelines to consider: CLAIM (Checklist for AI in Medical Imaging), TRIPOD+AI where applicable, and Metrics Reloaded [D11].
- A preprint (arXiv) is compatible with most of these venues. Check each venue's policy.
